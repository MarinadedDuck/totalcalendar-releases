TotalCalendar 0.2.1

  • TotalCalendar updated itself to this version. Later updates arrive the same way, and wait for your calendar changes to sync before restarting.
  • Your accounts, settings and calendar names carry over.
  • Nothing else changed since 0.2.0.

TotalCalendar 0.2.0

  • The first screen lets you choose where your calendars are: Google, Microsoft, iCloud, another CalDAV server, or the calendars on this Mac.
  • Calendars you can only view, such as holiday or sports subscriptions, say “View only” in the sidebar.
  • Undo moves, edits and deletes with Edit → Undo or the Undo button that appears after each change.
  • Move between events with Tab and the arrow keys, press Return to open one, jump with View → Go to Date…, and turn on week numbers.
  • Show a second time zone beside the hours, and see or change an event’s own time zone in its details.
  • Add your personal Zoom, Webex or other meeting link to events. Join buttons now also appear for links in an event’s location or notes.
  • Changes made offline keep trying until they reach your calendar, and any that couldn’t be sent can be retried or discarded from the toolbar.
  • TotalCalendar follows your Mac’s accent colour, and Help can show the welcome again or uninstall TotalCalendar.
